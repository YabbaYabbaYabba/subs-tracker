Settings

Appearance

System follows your iPhone's Light or Dark setting.

Defaults

Exchange rates

Optional. Enter rates by hand to see one combined total in your main currency. The app never goes online to fetch them.

Icons

Loads each service's icon from its own website, for subscriptions that have a Website and no photo or emoji icon. Your phone contacts those websites; nothing else is sent. Icons that a site lets the app copy are kept on this device; the rest are loaded from the site and cached for offline use.

Reminders

Creates calendar events with alerts for every active subscription, so your iPhone notifies you even when this app is closed.

Your data

Everything is stored only on this device. Back up now and then: deleting the app from the Home Screen or clearing Safari data erases it.
